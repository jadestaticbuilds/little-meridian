Little Meridian

A miniature city, grown one building at a time. Place homes, shops and parks so they lift each other, and watch the little city light up at dusk.

How to play

BUILD. Tap an empty plot, pick a building you can afford, and it rises. Tap a finished building to upgrade it or clear the plot.

COMBINE. Neighbours matter. Parks and schools help homes hold more people. Cafes and corner shops earn double beside homes or offices. A fountain plaza lifts the shops around it. Keep workshops away from homes.

GROW. Rent lands every dawn. Reach the population goal to complete the district and set the next one higher. There is no way to lose; the city grows at your pace.

Settings

Sound and motion for the little city.
